3. What is Filter()?
Array se sirf vahi element select krta h jo condition ko satisfy krte h or ek new array retun krta h.

let numbers = [10,15,20,25,30,35,40,45]
let even = numbers.Filter(num => num% 2 == 0)
console.log(even)
